Overview

Private Day Trip to Portofino, San Fruttuoso & Camogli from Genoa

We leave Genoa by ferry at 10:00 from Porto Antico and spend the day exploring Portofino, the hidden cove of San Fruttuoso, and the charming fishing village of Camogli — returning by the last ferry at 17:30. No tour bus, no fixed script. Just us, moving at our own pace. One of the best private day trips to Portofino from Genoa — and one of the few that includes San Fruttuoso and Camogli in a single day.

Portofino is popular and in high season you'll feel it. But there's a real difference between arriving on a coach tour with a fixed schedule and moving as your own private group — at your own pace, with someone who knows where to go, when to go, and which corners most visitors never reach.

Ferry — included

Golfo Paradiso ferry from Porto Antico. Scenic crossing along the Riviera.

Portofino

The iconic village seen with local eyes — the harbor, the viewpoints, the corners most tourists never reach.

San Fruttuoso Abbey

A medieval monastery in a hidden cove, reachable only by boat. Most Portofino day trips skip it entirely — we don't.

Camogli

A real Ligurian fishing village — colourful houses, pebble beach and focaccia worth travelling for. Most Portofino tours don't include Camogli. We do.

FAQ

Good to know

What time does it start and end?
We meet at Porto Antico at 09:30. Ferry departs at 10:00. Last ferry back from Camogli is at 17:30, arriving in Genoa around 18:30.
Are ferry tickets included?
Yes — all ferry tickets for the day are included in your price. The ferry is a public tourist boat (Golfo Paradiso) shared with other passengers, but we move independently.
Will Portofino be crowded?
In high season, yes — that's the honest answer. What makes a difference is moving as your own private group at your own pace, rather than being tied to a coach tour schedule. I know the timing and the less-visited paths that most day-trippers never find.
Is food included?
Food and drinks are not included unless agreed in advance. A full lunch at a local restaurant in Camogli can be added as an optional extra — just mention it when booking.
What if the weather is bad?
In rough weather, Golfo Paradiso may cancel or modify the service. I monitor conditions and will let you know in advance. Rescheduling with no penalty.
How does booking and payment work?
To confirm your date, a €100 deposit is required at the time of booking. The remaining balance is paid on the day of the experience in cash or by card. The deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 7 days before the scheduled experience. If you cancel less than 7 days before, the deposit is non-refundable. Rescheduling is possible whenever availability allows.
Can this work as a full-day excursion from the Genoa cruise port?
Yes — if your ship is docked for a full day, this is a great way to use it. We depart from Porto Antico at 10:00, which is easily reachable from the cruise terminal, and return to Genoa by 18:30. Check your ship's departure time before booking and let me know — I'll help you plan around it.
Is this suitable for families with children?
Yes, many of our guests are families. The ferry journey itself is a highlight for kids, and each stop offers something visually different — from Portofino's colorful harbor to the hidden cove of San Fruttuoso. The pace is flexible and fully adapted to your group.
