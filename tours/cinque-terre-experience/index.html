Overview

Private Cinque Terre Day Trip from Genoa

We take the train from Genoa and spend the day in Cinque Terre — exploring Vernazza, Manarola and Riomaggiore at a relaxed, unhurried pace. No big group schedule, no headset, no rushing. Just the villages as they actually are, with time for viewpoints, photos, local food, aperitivo and the small pauses that make the day feel real.

This private Cinque Terre tour from Genoa is designed for couples, friends, families and small groups who want the beauty of Cinque Terre without spending the day worrying about train times and crowded routes. In high season the crowds are real, but moving as your own private group makes the timing, pace and route much easier to adapt.

Train from Genoa

Direct regional train from Genova Brignole — about 1.5 hours, coastal views.

Vernazza, Manarola, Riomaggiore

Three villages, each with its own character. Explored properly, not rushed.

Cinque Terre Coastal Trail

Walk the famous hiking path between villages — we choose the best sections based on your group's fitness and preference on the day.

Local food stops

Anchovies, pesto, local wine — I'll point you to the right spots throughout the day.

Itinerary

How the day unfolds

Morning
Meeting at Genova Brignole station

We meet at the main entrance of Genova Brignole station. If you choose the ticket option, tickets are ready; otherwise we keep it simple and buy what you need on the day.

Genova Brignole · Main entrance
Open in Google Maps ↗
~1.5 hours
Train along the Ligurian coast

A scenic regional train ride. We use the time to choose the best rhythm for the villages, weather and crowds that day.

~2–3 hours
Vernazza

The most dramatic village — a natural harbour, a medieval tower and lanes winding up to panoramic views. Consistently rated the most beautiful of the five villages; arriving with a local host means finding the viewpoints and corners most visitors miss.

~1.5–2 hours
Manarola

One of the most beautiful villages in Italy. We time our arrival to get the best light.

~1 hour
Riomaggiore

The southernmost and slightly quieter village. Good wine, good views, a natural end to the day.

Optional · extra cost
Monterosso — beach & dinner

In summer, we can end the day in Monterosso — the only village with a proper beach. A swim, aperitivo, dinner at a local restaurant.

Summer only · extra cost · agree when booking
Evening
Train back to Genoa

We return together. I'll make sure you're on the right connection.

FAQ

Good to know

How is the price structured?
The price is per group, not per person — from €650 for up to 4 people. Train tickets are separate unless you choose the ticket option.
Are train tickets included?
It depends on the option. The base price does not include train tickets. The ticket option includes train tickets from Genoa to Cinque Terre and between villages, prepared in advance around the route we choose.
Will it be crowded?
In high season, yes — crowds are part of the reality. I know the timing, the less-visited paths and the quieter corners that make a real difference.
Is food included?
Food and drinks are not included unless agreed in advance. I'll point you to curated local spots throughout the day.
Can we add Monterosso and dinner?
Yes — in summer (June–September) we can end in Monterosso with a swim and dinner. Extra cost, arranged in advance. Mention it when booking.
How does booking and payment work?
You can pay the full amount securely through Stripe or reserve with a €100 deposit. If you choose the deposit, the remaining balance is paid online via a secure payment link before the experience. The deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 48 hours before the scheduled experience. If you cancel less than 48 hours before, the deposit is non-refundable. Rescheduling is possible whenever availability allows.
Is this tour suitable for families with children?
Yes — many of our guests are families with kids. The pace is fully flexible, and the train journey from Genoa (1.5 hours along the coast) is a highlight in itself. For younger children, we skip the coastal hike and focus on village exploration instead. I adapt the day to what works best for your group.
Is there a lot of hiking? Is it physically demanding?
Only if you want it to be. The coastal trail hike is optional — some sections are moderate, others are easy. If your group prefers to stick to village streets and take the train between stops, we do exactly that. The experience adapts entirely to your fitness level and preferences on the day.
Is this good for friends or small private groups?
Yes. This is a private day trip, so it works beautifully for friends, couples, families and small groups. We can make the route more scenic, more relaxed, more food-focused, or more photo-friendly depending on your group.
