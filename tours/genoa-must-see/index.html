Overview

Genoa is simple, but not obvious

This private experience is a relaxed way to discover the real Genoa with me: hidden streets, panoramic viewpoints, local food recommendations and the parts of the city that are easy to miss on your own.

You'll see the landmarks that matter, but also the quieter corners most visitors never reach. Small piazzas hidden behind stone archways, rooftop viewpoints above the city, local cafés, unexpected details and the everyday side of Genoa that makes people fall in love with it.

When I first moved to Genoa, I didn't understand why locals loved it so much. It took years of walking, exploring and getting lost before I began to notice what makes it special. More than ten years later, the city still surprises me.

Some of the best views are hidden above the rooftops, some of the best food is easy to miss, and some of the most beautiful corners are only a few steps from the busy streets. That's what I love sharing.

There is no rush and no fixed script. I show you Genoa as I would show it to a friend visiting from abroad — with the right mix of highlights, hidden corners, local stories and personal recommendations, shaped around your pace and interests.

Itinerary

A sample route, not a checklist

Meeting point

The standard meeting point is in the historic center at Via della Mercanzia 2. If you are staying centrally or arriving by cruise, we can confirm the most convenient meeting point after booking.

Via della Mercanzia 2 · Historic center · Near Porto Antico
Open in Google Maps ↗
Via Balbi & Basilica della Nunziata

A grand avenue lined with historic palaces, leading to one of Genoa's most dazzling churches — a sober façade hiding an interior covered in gold and frescoes.

Piazza Truogoli di Santa Brigida

A hidden little square with old communal washing basins — a glimpse of everyday Genoa that most visitors walk right past.

Via Garibaldi & the Rolli Palaces

The UNESCO-listed "Street of Kings" — a row of aristocratic palaces, including Via Garibaldi 12, where Genoa's wealthiest families once hosted royalty.

Belvedere Castelletto

Up via the charming Art Nouveau Liberty lift to a terrace with one of the best panoramas over the rooftops, domes and harbor.

Piazza De Ferrari

Genoa's grand main square around its great bronze fountain — the lively heart of the city, framed by the opera house and elegant palaces.

Chiesa del Gesù

A baroque jewel just off the main square, home to two original Rubens masterpieces that most visitors never realize are there.

Porta Soprana & Casa di Colombo

The towering medieval city gate and, right beside it, the boyhood house of Christopher Columbus.

San Lorenzo Cathedral

Genoa's striking striped-marble cathedral — one of the oldest in Italy, full of stories most tourists never hear, including an unexploded WWII shell still kept inside.

Caruggi & local food stop

We wander the vicoli — the narrow medieval alleyways full of real local life — and pause for food or coffee if you feel like it.

Porto Antico

We finish by the sea at the revitalized old harbor — the perfect moment for your last questions and personal tips for the rest of your stay.

The exact path changes with the weather, your pace and what feels right on the day. Half Day focuses on the essential old town and a panoramic viewpoint. Full Day lets us add the sea-facing Genoa: Boccadasse, Corso Italia or Nervi, more views and a slower local rhythm.

FAQ

Good to know

Where do we meet?
Meeting point details shared after booking. I adapt to your location — hotel, cruise terminal or anywhere central. If you're arriving at the Genoa cruise port (Terminal Crociere), I'll meet you directly there.
How many people can join?
This is a private experience — just your group, no strangers. Couples, friends, families and small private groups work beautifully. The booking page supports groups up to 12 guests total.
Is food included?
Food and drinks are paid separately, so the day stays flexible. I will suggest the best local stops for focaccia, pesto, farinata, gelato, seafood or vegetarian Genovese dishes, and you choose what you actually want to try.
Can I customize the route?
Absolutely. If you want more food, more viewpoints, more hidden streets, a slower family pace, or extra neighborhoods such as Boccadasse or Nervi, mention it when booking and I'll adapt the walk.
Can we make it a full day?
Yes, and I recommend it if you have the time. The full-day version lets us see the essential old town, then add the sea-facing side of Genoa: Boccadasse, Corso Italia, Nervi, more viewpoints, slower food stops and a more local rhythm. You can book it online the same way as the half-day walk.
What's the cancellation policy?
The €100 deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 48 hours before the scheduled experience. If you cancel less than 48 hours before, the deposit is non-refundable. Rescheduling is possible whenever availability allows.
How does booking and payment work?
Choose Half Day or Full Day, then select your date, start time and group size. You can pay the full amount securely through Stripe or reserve with a €100 deposit. During booking, you can share your cruise timing, pace, children, food preferences or mobility needs. I will then confirm your booking and send the exact meeting point and any useful details before the experience.
Is this a traditional historical tour?
Not exactly. These experiences are designed as relaxed local walks — the kind of day I'd naturally share with friends visiting Genoa. The focus is on atmosphere, hidden corners, food, views and authentic local culture rather than formal historical guiding. For guests curious about Genoa's history and cultural context, I also share a curated digital booklet with stories, facts and recommendations before the experience.
Is this good for cruise ship passengers stopping in Genoa?
Yes — this Genoa shore excursion is one of the easiest ways to enjoy the city during a cruise stop. The half-day walk runs 3.5 hours, I can meet you at the cruise terminal or city center, and you'll be back comfortably before your ship departs. Just send your ship's schedule when you book and I'll plan around it.
Is this suitable for families with children or older travelers?
Absolutely. Children and teenagers are very welcome. I adapt the pace, add breaks when needed and point out things that might interest them. At the same time, this is a relaxed city walk for the whole family, not a kids' activity. Teenagers especially may enjoy it in their own way, and that's completely fine. Parents don't need to make them listen to every detail; you are here to enjoy your time in Genoa too.
