Overview

Private Walking Tour of Genoa's Old Town

Walk through the heart of the city with a local host, discover hidden streets, hear fascinating stories and enjoy authentic local tastings. A perfect introduction to Genoa — at your own pace, with no group and no rush. Ideal as a private experience in Genoa's old town, a shore excursion for cruise passengers with a few hours in port, or a family-friendly introduction to the city for first-time visitors.

Iconic landmarks & hidden gems

UNESCO-listed Via Garibaldi, the Rolli Palaces, the Caruggi medieval alleyways and the Christopher Columbus birthplace — seen with local eyes.

Taste authentic local food

Focaccia, fresh pesto, farinata — I'll point you to the right spots and you choose what to try.

Local stories, not boring history

Learn the city's stories in a relaxed and engaging way.

Local tips & recommendations

Restaurant picks, viewpoints, what to do next — especially useful if you're in Genoa for just one day or arriving by cruise ship.

Itinerary

A typical route

Start
Meeting point

We meet in the city center. Exact location confirmed after booking — flexible to your accommodation.

~20 min
Via Balbi & Basilica della Nunziata

A grand avenue lined with historic palaces, leading to one of Genoa's most dazzling churches — a sober façade hiding an interior covered in gold and frescoes.

~15 min
Piazza Truogoli di Santa Brigida

A hidden little square with old communal washing basins — a glimpse of everyday Genoa that most visitors walk right past.

~30 min
Via Garibaldi & the Rolli Palaces

The UNESCO-listed "Street of Kings" — a row of aristocratic palaces, including Via Garibaldi 12, where Genoa's wealthiest families once hosted royalty.

~20 min
Belvedere Castelletto

Up via the charming Art Nouveau Liberty lift to a terrace with one of the best panoramas over the rooftops, domes and harbor.

~20 min
Piazza De Ferrari

Genoa's grand main square around its great bronze fountain — the lively heart of the city, framed by the opera house and elegant palaces.

~20 min
Chiesa del Gesù

A baroque jewel just off the main square, home to two original Rubens masterpieces that most visitors never realize are there.

~20 min
Porta Soprana & Casa di Colombo

The towering medieval city gate and, right beside it, the boyhood house of Christopher Columbus.

~30 min
San Lorenzo Cathedral

Genoa's striking striped-marble cathedral — one of the oldest in Italy, full of stories most tourists never hear, including an unexploded WWII shell still kept inside.

~30 min
Caruggi & local food stop

We wander the vicoli — the narrow medieval alleyways full of real local life — and stop at carefully chosen spots for authentic Genoese flavors. You choose what to try, no pressure.

End
Porto Antico

We finish by the sea at the revitalized old harbor — the perfect moment for your last questions and personal tips for the rest of your stay.

This is a typical route, not a fixed script. The exact path, stops and timing can shift depending on the weather, your mood and energy on the day — so not every detail is listed here. What never changes: together we see the essential heart of Genoa, the places everyone should experience at least once, plus the hidden gems and local tastings we discover along the way.

FAQ

Good to know

Where do we meet?
Meeting point details shared after booking. I adapt to your location — hotel, cruise terminal or anywhere central. If you're arriving at the Genoa cruise port (Terminal Crociere), I'll meet you directly there.
How many people can join?
This is a private experience — just your group, no strangers. Groups of 1–8 people work great.
Is food included?
Food and drinks are not included unless agreed in advance. I'll point you to curated local spots and you choose what you'd like to try.
Can I customize the route?
Absolutely. If you have specific interests, just mention it when booking and I'll adapt the route.
What's the cancellation policy?
The €100 deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 7 days before the scheduled experience. If you cancel less than 7 days before, the deposit is non-refundable. Rescheduling is possible whenever availability allows.
How does booking and payment work?
To confirm your date, a €100 deposit is required at the time of booking. The remaining balance is paid on the day of the experience in cash or by card. The deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 7 days before the scheduled experience. If you cancel less than 7 days before, the deposit is non-refundable. Rescheduling is possible whenever availability allows.
Is this a traditional historical tour?
Not exactly. These experiences are designed as relaxed local walks — the kind of day I'd naturally share with friends visiting Genoa. The focus is on atmosphere, hidden corners, food, views and authentic local culture rather than formal historical guiding. For guests curious about Genoa's history and cultural context, I also share a curated digital booklet with stories, facts and recommendations before the experience.
Is this good for cruise ship passengers stopping in Genoa?
Yes — this is one of the most popular choices for cruise ship passengers docking at the Genoa cruise terminal. The tour runs 3 hours, I meet you at the port or city center, and you'll be back comfortably before your ship departs. Just let me know your ship's schedule when you book and I'll plan around it.
Is this suitable for families with children or older travelers?
Absolutely. The route is an easy walk with no strenuous sections and plenty of stops. I adapt the pace and storytelling to who's in the group — whether that's kids who need short bursts of engagement or older travelers who prefer a slower, more relaxed pace. Many of our guests are families and retirees.
